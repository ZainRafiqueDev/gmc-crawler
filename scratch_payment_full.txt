Payment Policy
CUSTOMER TRANSPARENCY

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

At Vellano, we are committed to providing a secure, smooth, and reliable payment experience. This policy outlines the payment methods we accept, how transactions are processed, and important billing details. By placing an order on our website, you agree to the terms outlined below.

Accepted Payment Methods

We offer a variety of convenient, encrypted payment options at checkout. Available methods include:

Credit & Debit Cards

Processed securely via Stripe. We accept major cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. Card details are fully tokenized and encrypted.

PayPal

Pay quickly and securely using your PayPal balance, linked bank account, or stored credit/debit cards with buyer protection.

Buy Now, Pay Later

Flexible installment plans through Affirm & Afterpay. Split your purchase into manageable payments at checkout subject to eligibility.

Digital Wallets

One-touch, secure checkout options including Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay on supported devices and browsers.

Secure Transactions

Your payment security is our absolute priority. All transactions are processed through encrypted payment gateways (including Stripe and PayPal) that strictly comply with Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI-DSS Level 1). Vellano does not collect, store, or have access to your full card numbers or sensitive financial credentials on our servers.

Payment Authorization & Order Confirmation

When you place an order, your selected payment method is authorized immediately for the full total. If authorization fails, your order cannot be processed.

Once your transaction succeeds, an automated Order Confirmation Email containing your receipt and item summary is sent immediately. If you do not see it within a few minutes, please check your spam folder or contact our support team.

Pricing, Currency & Taxes

All prices on our store are listed in USD ($). While prices are subject to change without notice, your purchase price remains locked and fixed once an order has been successfully confirmed.

Applicable sales taxes are calculated automatically during checkout based on your delivery address in accordance with state and local laws. For any international shipments, customers are responsible for applicable import duties or local fees.

Failed or Declined Transactions

If your payment is declined, it is usually due to one of the following reasons:

Incorrect billing address, CVV code, or expiration date.
Insufficient account funds or credit line limits.
Card issuer automated fraud prevention filters.
Temporary network or gateway timeouts.

Please double-check your billing details, try an alternative payment method, or contact your issuing bank for assistance.

Refunds & Fraud Prevention

All approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method used during checkout (e.g., directly to your credit card, PayPal balance, or installment account). Refunds typically take 3 to 5 business days to reflect on your statement. For return conditions and eligibility, please review our Return & Refund Policy.

To protect our customers against fraudulent transactions, we reserve the right to verify or cancel orders flagged as suspicious prior to shipment.

Need Help with Billing or Payments?

Our customer care team is available 24/7 to assist with payment questions, invoices, and checkout support.
