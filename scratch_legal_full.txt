Compliance with U.S. Federal & State Regulations

We actively monitor and adhere to the latest consumer protection and digital commerce policies published by the U.S. government. Our operations are governed by the following frameworks:

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) & CPRA: As a business located in California, we strictly respect your right to consumer privacy. We do not sell your personal data. You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal information at any time. For full details on data handling, please read our Privacy Policy.
Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Guidelines: Our pricing, advertising, and promotional practices strictly follow FTC regulations to prevent deceptive marketing. All product descriptions for our car accessories are written to be as accurate and factual as possible.
Consumer Review Fairness Act (CRFA): We value authentic customer feedback. In compliance with the CRFA, we will never penalize a customer for leaving an honest review of our products, nor do we artificially restrict genuine public feedback regarding our car accessories.
